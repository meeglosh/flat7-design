Estateably

Mike Jerugim, case study · Estateably · Director of Design

Estate settlement is one of the most fragmented, manual, and emotionally charged workflows there is.

We set out to rebuild it as one secure, compliant network.

It starts with a family.

Executors and beneficiaries inherit a process that has always been opaque: handoffs across institutions, repeated document requests, unclear ownership, constant delays.

Before formal interviews, I ran an initial wireframing pass so leadership had something tangible to react to.

Eighty hours in the trenches.

0+ hours

We interviewed professional estate agents and service providers to learn how settlement really works, then mapped how every document moves.

The infographic behind this world.

Mid-fidelity, back in users' hands.

An interactive prototype with prospective users validated core usability and pressure-tested the end-to-end experience before any visual polish.

Then high fidelity.

Visual hierarchy, accessibility, trust signalling, and consistency across a fast-growing platform, for people often using it under stress.

A dashboard for the CRA.

Agents at the Canada Revenue Agency process estate files inside Estateably: approval, rejection, and audit flagging in a secure, compliant environment.

Beneficiary Concierge.

It gave executors and beneficiaries real-time visibility into the estates they were part of. For many, the first clear view of a process that had always felt inaccessible.

Lifebook.

Lifebook let people organise their affairs in advance: inventory, accounts, passwords, records.

One network. Every participant, one ledger, no more handoffs in the dark.
