What is Wingman?

  • Wingman is a geeky side project I built with AI tools.
  • It’s a presentation tool for people who think on their feet.
  • Describe your topic and it builds the first slide. Then you just talk, and the deck grows as you go.
Composing next slide
Photo: Unsplash

Why I built it

  • It started with a conversation with a friend. We both make up our presentations as we go.
  • As a musician, I’ve always been drawn to improvisation.
  • PowerPoint and Slides want the deck finished before you open your mouth. I wanted the opposite.
Composing next slide
Photo: Unsplash

How it works

  • Speech-to-slide in real time. Say “next slide” to move on.
  • AI-matched photo backgrounds, so every slide looks designed.
  • When you’re done, export to PowerPoint or Google Slides, or share it as a link. Edit every slide after the fact.

What I learned

  • Designing the empty state matters more when the content writes itself.
  • A live tool has to feel calm. Every animation is there to say “I heard you.”
  • I went in as a product designer and came out having shipped a full-stack, paid product on my own: auth, billing, a real-time AI pipeline, the lot.

Building software solo is a design skill now.
