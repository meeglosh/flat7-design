Who it is for

Heidi Rood, and the people she teaches.

Heidi is an Anat Baniel Method NeuroMovement practitioner for children and adults. Her clients book for themselves or for their children, in person or online. Public contact is by email only, and no street address is published.

What I learned, one

Calm on top is earned underneath.

The client sees three gentle steps: find a time, pay, confirmed. Beneath them sit payment, booking, refunds, credits and approvals.

Moment one · See the times

Find a time.

Group class times are public on the sign-in page, before anyone has made an account. Tuesdays 7 to 8 p.m. and Wednesdays noon to 1 p.m. Eastern, from October 13, 2026. Up to 50 seats, for adults and children 12+.

What I learned, two

Show the times before asking for anything.

Group class times are public before sign-in. Nobody should have to hand over an email address to find out when the class is.

Moment two · Pay, then book

Pay first. Then the lesson is booked.

A virtual private lesson is CAD $40, paid by card at booking. The lesson is placed in Cal.com only after Stripe confirms the payment.

  • The browser return and the webhook can arrive in either order.
  • Either one settles it. The booking happens once.

What I learned, three

Fail toward the client.

If the time is taken before the lesson is booked, the payment is refunded automatically.

If a checkout is paid twice, it is refunded automatically.

If Heidi rejects a booking, it is refunded.

Nobody has to notice, or ask.

Moment three · The right amount of friction

Friction where it protects people.

In-person sessions need an intake form, for adults and children, reviewed every six months. Virtual sessions skip it: the client ticks a box agreeing to the Medical Disclaimer and Cancellation Policy, and the server enforces and records it.

  • In person: intake form, reviewed every six months.
  • Virtual: one checkbox, recorded by the server.
  • A child’s first private booking: approved by Heidi. Later ones go straight through.

What I learned, four

Policies are product.

Every launch question went to Heidi. Her answers are kept as a written record, and each answer became a rule in the system.

24 hours

Cancel at least 24 hours ahead and the lesson is refunded, or the class credit comes back, automatically. Later, it is forfeited unless Heidi waives it.

Vermont, days 1 to 7

In-person sessions run Monday to Friday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern. Vermont on days 1 to 7 of each month, Montreal the rest. Virtual private lessons run 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.

First booking approval

Only a child’s first private booking needs Heidi’s approval. Later ones go straight through.

Heidi’s view

Her side is quiet too.

Heidi signs in and sees two staff tools: Review intake and Return class credits. Credits from cancellations made 24 hours ahead are returned automatically, so the list only holds the cases that need her judgment.

Privacy

Quiet by default.

Meeting titles are neutral, so calendar invites never expose who booked.
