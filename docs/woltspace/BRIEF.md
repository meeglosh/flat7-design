## Grammar and journey
Chaptered editorial, with a single interactive exhibit as the peak. This is a case study about design judgment, not a product landing page. Plain in-flow contents links; no fixed folio. Title page first; first media begins below opening title. Media has its own ground and captions. No scrims, no magnetic controls, no film, no persistent device frame. Closing credit plate includes a prototype link and portfolio return.
Other grammars: live surface confuses case study with product; filmic and continuous world obscure design reasoning; gallery has no causal argument; poster underuses real interface; split stage implies an unsupported historical before/after; cutlist rushes technical comprehension.
